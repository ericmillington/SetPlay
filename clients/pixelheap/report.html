What we found

HighDiscoverability

Searchers can't find you by name or by studio

Across 5 searches (your name, name + designer, name + town, 'Pixelheap' + service + town, and the domain itself), none returned your site or a profile of yours. Results were namesakes, unrelated 'pixel' studios and local competitors. Note: this snapshot used one search engine; we'll confirm in signed-out Google from your area.

Evidence: Search snapshot, 2026-11-08

HighDiscoverability

Your name isn't in the home page title

The title reads 'Senior Designer - Pixelheap.Design - Brand & Web Design - Clifton Park, NY' (74 characters, so it gets truncated). The H1 is 'Eric Millington', but the title is what search engines weigh. Suggested title: 'Eric Millington · Brand & Web Designer, Clifton Park NY | Pixelheap'.

Evidence: site-check.json → seo.title

HighConsistency

Three different stories about who you are

The home page says 'a design studio led by a senior art director'. The portfolio says 'I'm a front-end developer with a background in print design'. The quote page mixes 'I' and 'us'. A prospect reading two pages gets two people. Pick one positioning and one voice, then write a canonical bio (short/medium/long) and reuse it everywhere.

Evidence: /, /millington-design-portfolio/, /get-a-free-quote/

MediumConsistency

'20+ years' and '25+ years' on the same page

The hero says 20+ years and the About section says 25+. Both are true-ish; only one should appear.

Evidence: Home page hero vs. 'About Eric Millington'

MediumPrivacy & Safety

Phone number on every page; the quote form requires one

'Call or text: 518.229.3364' appears in the content and footer site-wide, so scrapers will harvest it. If it's your personal cell, use a business line (Google Voice, OpenPhone). The quote form also makes phone number required; making it optional reduces friction and collects less data.

Evidence: Home footer; /get-a-free-quote/ form

MediumPrivacy & Safety

Google tag loads with no consent notice

Site Kit injects Google's gtag (GT-PJ43BXS) on every page. For a US studio this is common, but it conflicts with the privacy-first story you'll be selling. Use cookieless analytics (Plausible or Fathom), or add a proper consent banner.

Evidence: googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=GT-PJ43BXS

MediumOwnership & Control

Unclaimed profiles where clients look

No Google Business Profile, Behance, Dribbble or chamber listing was found. Local competitors (3tone Digital, Clever Coding) rank through exactly these. 'Heapix.design' already exists on Dribbble, so reserve 'pixelheap' on major platforms now.

Evidence: Search snapshot

MediumWebsite Quality

Two form plugins and 21 scripts on the home page

Contact Form 7 and WPForms both load, including WPForms' address field, input-mask and country list, on a page with no form. Load forms only on the quote page and remove the unused plugin.

Evidence: site-check.json → script_count: 21

MediumWebsite Quality

No security headers

HSTS, Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy and Permissions-Policy are all missing. That's a few lines in .htaccess on Apache.

Evidence: site-check.json → security_headers

MediumReputation & Content

Great client list, no social proof

GlobalFoundries, SPAC and the Freihofer's Jazz Festival are a strong list, but there are no testimonials, no reviews and no results ('traffic up X%'). Ask 3 past clients for two sentences each.

Evidence: Home & portfolio pages

LowReputation & Content

Blog quiet for nearly a year

The last post was Nov 23, 2025. The voice is great ('Build your website for a doom-scrolling goldfish'). Even one post a month on topics clients search for would help discoverability.

Evidence: /ye-olde-pixels/

LowWebsite Quality

Small hygiene items

Headings skip from H2 to H4. The LinkedIn link is written 'Https://'. The quote page has no meta description. An auto-titled page (/1739-2/) is still published.

Evidence: site-check.json; home.html
