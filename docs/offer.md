# The SetPlay Offer

## Positioning

Most people's online presence was never designed. It built up by accident: a LinkedIn
from 2014, an abandoned Instagram, a Squarespace site with a dead contact form, a
data broker listing your home address, and a stranger who ranks above you for
your own name.

SetPlay turns that accidental footprint into an intentional one.

**One-liner:** *We find everything the internet says about you, help you clean
it up, and build you a home online you're proud to send people to.*

**Who it's for (in order of likely fit):**

1. **Independent professionals**: consultants, coaches, therapists, realtors, lawyers, advisors. Their name *is* the brand.
2. **Creatives and makers**: artists, photographers, writers, musicians.
3. **Small local businesses**: a single owner-operator with a scattered presence.
4. **People in transition**: job seekers, founders, people re-entering public life, people starting a public-facing role.

**What makes it different:**

- **Audit first, website second.** We don't sell a website until we know what it has to fix.
- **Responsible by default.** The sites are privacy-respecting, accessible, fast, and owned by the client (see `principles.md`).
- **A score people understand.** The 0–100 Presence Score across six pillars makes the value visible and gives a reason to re-audit.

session referral, profile copywriting ($75/profile), Google Business Profile setup, a
"handle reservation" sweep.

**Pricing logic:** The audit fee is credited toward a Presence Build if booked within 30
days. The audit acts as a paid discovery phase, so it should lead naturally into the build.

## Process and timeline (Presence Build)

|---|---|
| 0 | Discovery call → intake + consent form signed → deposit (50%) |
| 1 | Footprint audit → report walkthrough call → roadmap agreed |
| 1–2 | Cleanup sprint: profile updates, account consolidation, broker opt-outs, 2FA |

| 2–3 | Site design + build → one revision round |
| 4 | Launch → re-score → handoff (credentials, how-to video) → balance due |

## What we will NOT do (say this on the sales page)

- Audit someone without their written consent.
- Access private accounts, guess passwords, or use leaked data beyond breach-*exposure* checks.
- Suppress legitimate news or reviews through deceptive means, fake reviews, or astroturfing.
- Lock the client in: they own the domain, the code, and every account.
